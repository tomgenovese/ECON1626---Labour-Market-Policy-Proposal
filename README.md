# ECON1626 Assignment 2 — AI and the Australian Labour Market

This repository contains an evidence-based policy proposal advising the **Department of Employment and Workplace Relations (DEWR)** on how Australia should respond to the emerging risk that generative AI may weaken entry-level employment pathways in highly exposed occupations.

The proposal evaluates three policy options and recommends a targeted monitoring and workforce adjustment approach.

## Repository contents

* **[`Proposal.MD`](Proposal.MD)** — 1,200-word policy proposal, including analysis, policy options, recommendations, implementation plan and references.
* **[`Reflection.MD`](Reflection.MD)** — 200-word reflection on the use of AI during the assessment process.
