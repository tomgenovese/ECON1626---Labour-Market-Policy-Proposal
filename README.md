# ECON1626 Assignment 2 — AI and the Australian Labour Market
 
An evidence-based policy brief advising the Department of Employment and Workplace Relations (DEWR) on Australia's early AI-related hiring slowdown. It argues the measured gap between employment growth in high- and low-exposure occupations is real but fragile, operates through suppressed hiring rather than displacement, and faces an immediate institutional problem: the quarterly monitoring series DEWR plans cannot resume until late 2026 because of the ABS occupational classification transition, and carries no decision rule when it does. The brief recommends securing the series through that break and attaching a threshold that activates a capped, pre-legislated entry-level hiring subsidy.
 
**Policy context:** Responds to *AI and employment in Australia: Monitoring framework and evidence to date* (DEWR Office of the Chief Economist, 8 July 2026).
